## Identity sheet

Sixteen dogs in the same green/cream kit and headband. Use this for the FitFight character recipe: cream paper, graphite line, moss kit, standing pose, painted ground, no leaderboard.

## Five forms

Read left to right as form **1 → 5**. Clothes and body change; face, species, and accessories stay.

| Form | Turtle / raccoon / Maine Coon | Goat hike variant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Armchair, blanket, mug | Same |
| 2 | Up, walking, still soft (hoodie or easy tank) | Walking, no pack |
| 3 | Walking with kit / pack | Pack + bottle |
| 4 | Jogging, athletic | Hiking poles |
| 5 | Sprinting, more muscular | Summit pose |

FitFight is Steps, not climbing. Use the **turtle/raccoon/cat ladder** as the product forms. Keep the goat sheet as “same character, five poses,” not as a second sport.

## Group scenes

Small named casts (about 3–9 animals), cream paper, moss kit, one activity. Names on caps are a study choice. Live FitFight should keep **usernames and scores outside the PNG** so standings cannot fight the drawing.

Trail-run is closest to Steps. Swim / climb / fence are composition studies, not fight types.

## What not to copy

- Do not treat these PNGs as the 12 stock animals already in the app.
- Do not invent Feed posts from them.
- Do not send HealthKit totals, ranks, or loser-action text into Blend because a study drew a name on a cap.
