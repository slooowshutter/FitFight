Who is looking

Likes, dislikes and comments are signed with this name. Each person sets it once on their own phone or computer. On a shared computer, tap a name below to switch.

Shortcuts

Click a key, then press the new one. If another action already uses it, the two swap. Esc cancels. Saved on this device.

Where your data lives

  • Shared file: docs/design/source/kit/glowup-state.json in the FitFight repo on the Mac that runs the server. It holds everyone's likes, dislikes and comments. Agents read it when you say "read my choices". Commit it to keep a history.
  • This browser: a copy in localStorage under glowup.state, plus glowup.pending (changes made while the server was off, sent automatically next time), glowup.who (your name), glowup.keys (your shortcuts) and glowup.theme.
  • Server: python3 docs/design/source/kit/glowup-serve.py from the repo, then open http://localhost:8765. It prints a Wi-Fi address for phones on the same network.
